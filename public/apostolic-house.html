What This Pillar Is

A House Before It Is a Meeting

Most people encounter church as an event they attend. Scripture presents something far weightier: a household with a father, a family, an inheritance and a governmental assignment. The Apostolic House is the first pillar of Holy Nation Global because everything else — the college, the prayer towers, the networks, the nation-building — is born out of a healthy house.

Two words shape everything we do here. Oikos, the household of God, describes our life together: belonging, fathering, discipline, provision and inheritance. Ecclesia, the called-out governing assembly, describes our function: we do not merely worship, we legislate the will of heaven over the territories entrusted to us.

“… that you may know how you ought to conduct yourself in the house of God, which is the church of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth.” 1 Timothy 3:15

Why the house comes first

A gift can carry a person into a room that their character cannot keep them in. The house exists to close that gap. Before we send anyone into the twelve spheres, we father them, feed them, correct them and commission them. What is not formed in the house cannot be sustained in the field.

  • Sound doctrine — the foundational teaching of Christ, laid deliberately and in order
  • Spiritual fathering — every believer known, and no leader left unaccountable
  • Consistent rhythm — weekly gatherings, daily altars and the seven appointed feasts
  • Commissioning — the house sends; it does not hoard

The Seven Feasts of Christ

Beyond the weekly rhythm, the house observes seven appointed seasons through the year. Each feast carries its own teaching emphasis, fast and corporate assignment.

01 — The Feast of Beginnings

Opens the year with consecration, corporate fasting and the release of the house's prophetic mandate for the season. Every member sets personal and family altars.

02 — The Feast of the Word

A concentrated season of teaching, Scripture memorisation and doctrinal grounding. Global College enrolment traditionally opens during this feast.

03 — The Feast of the Cross

Centred on the passion, death and resurrection of Christ. Marked by repentance, reconciliation within the house, and the widest public evangelism of the year.

04 — The Feast of the Spirit

Pentecost season. Impartation, gifts, commissioning of new ministers and the sending of mission teams into the field.

05 — The Feast of the Harvest

Thanksgiving, first-fruits giving, and the funding of the compassion and nation-building arms for the coming cycle.

06 — The Feast of the Kingdom

Aligned with the Kingdom Congress. The house turns its attention outward to governance, economy and the twelve spheres of influence.

07 — The Feast of the Return

Closes the year with the doctrine of Christ's return, accountability reviews for every leader, and the sealing of the year's assignments.

The Mentoring Pathway

Step 01

Application & Conversation

You submit a short application and meet with a member of the mentoring team. We are listening for where you are, not testing whether you qualify.

Step 02

Placement

You are matched with a mentor by track, season and sphere. Where relevant, mentors are matched by gender and life stage.

Step 03

Covenant & Rhythm

Both parties agree a written rhythm — frequency, confidentiality, accountability and what success looks like over the term.

Step 04

Formation & Assignment

Mentoring runs alongside real assignment. You are given something to carry, and mentored through carrying it.

Step 05

Review & Commissioning

At the close of the term, the council reviews growth and either commissions, extends, or redirects — honestly and without shame.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I have to become a member to attend?

No. You are welcome at any gathering for as long as you like without joining anything. Membership exists for those who want covenant relationship and accountability — it is offered, never pressed.

I live outside Nigeria. Can I still belong?

Yes. We run online house fellowships across several time zones, and the Morning Altar and Sunday broadcast are both live. Many of our partners and mentees have never set foot in the Abuja centre.

Is giving required for partnership?

Friend-level partnership carries no financial requirement. Covenant Partner and Kingdom Builder involve consistent giving, because those tiers fund specific work — and we publish an annual report showing exactly where it went.

What do you believe?

We hold to historic Christian orthodoxy, and our four articles of belief are published in full on the About page: the supreme lordship of Christ over all government, the coming of His Kingdom, the call to colonise earth with the ideals of that Kingdom, and the manifestation of the sons of God.

How is leadership held accountable?

Leadership is plural. Every leader, including the president, sits under a council with authority to review conduct, doctrine and finances. Quarterly character reviews apply to everyone in the mentoring structure without exception.

Can my church partner with the house?

Yes. Congregational and ministerial affiliation runs through the International Fellowship of Ministers. See the Networks pillar for the affiliation pathway.
