The Communities

What the WhatsApp Groups Are For

Every nation has one main community group, plus city groups where numbers justify them. They exist to make a global movement feel local — and to make sure nobody who joins us disappears quietly a month later.

What you will get

  • The daily Morning Altar reminder, in your own time zone
  • Weekly gathering details for your city and the national online meeting
  • Prayer requests from your nation, and answers when they come
  • Teaching links, conference registration and Global College intake notices
  • A direct line to your national coordinator — a real person with a name

House rules

Every community is moderated, and the rules are the same everywhere. They are short, and we enforce them.

  • No selling, MLM, fundraising or job adverts — not even good causes
  • No party politics, and no campaigning of any kind
  • No forwarded prophecies, chain messages or unverified alarm
  • No direct-messaging members you have not met, especially women and minors
  • Disagree freely; contempt gets you removed
  • Under-18s belong in the TANI groups, which are separately moderated

Safety & Privacy

  • Group admins are named and accountable to the national coordinator
  • Your number is visible to other members — join knowing that
  • We never ask for money, bank details or personal documents on WhatsApp
  • Nobody from this movement will ever DM you asking for a payment
  • Under-18 groups have two cleared adult admins and no private messaging
  • Report anything uncomfortable to the coordinator or to safeguarding

If someone contacts you claiming to represent Holy Nation and asks for money, it is a scam. Report it to theholynationglobal@gmail.com.

Not on the Map?

Start Holy Nation in Your Country

Every one of the twelve communities above began the same way: two or three people in a country with no presence, praying about it and writing to us. Not a strategy meeting in Lagos.

You do not need to be a pastor, own a building, or have a group already. You need to be willing to pray consistently and to be known by name.

What starting looks like

  • Month 1 — you write to us; we talk, pray and get to know you
  • Month 2–3 — you join an online house fellowship and begin a watch
  • Month 4–6 — a national WhatsApp community opens with you as first contact
  • Month 6–12 — regular online gathering, Foundations Class, first in-person meeting
  • Year 2 — a national page, a coordinator, and the full five-year TKC establishment cycle

We will not announce your country on this site until there is something real behind the name. That is a promise about our honesty, not a hurdle for you.

Questions

Frequently Asked

My country isn't listed. Can I still be part of this?

Yes. The online house fellowships run across every time zone and the Morning Altar is live daily. Write to us and we will place you in the nearest community — and if you want to, help you begin something where you are.

Is there a cost to join a national community?

None. Communities, chapters, prayer watches and the Foundations Class are all free. Conferences carry a delegate fee to cover venue and catering, with bursaries for anyone who cannot meet it.

Will joining a WhatsApp group mean constant messages?

No. Groups are moderated and mostly quiet: a daily altar reminder, weekly gathering details and prayer requests. Forwards, selling and chain messages are removed, and repeat offenders are removed with them.

Do I have to leave my current church?

No, and we would discourage it. Most people in these communities belong to local churches with no formal relationship to Holy Nation Global. We are built to sit alongside your congregation, not to replace it.

Someone messaged me asking for money in your name. Is that you?

No. Nobody from this movement will ever direct-message you asking for a payment, bank details or personal documents. If it happens, it is a scam — screenshot it and send it to theholynationglobal@gmail.com so we can warn the community.

Can my teenager join a community group?

Under-18s belong in the TANI groups, which are separately moderated with two cleared adult admins and no private messaging. A parent or guardian should make the first contact. See the Networks pillar for the full safeguarding position.

How do I give, and how do I know where it goes?

Payment integration is being finalised. When it is live, every arm publishes annual income and expenditure, partners receive a report on what their giving funded, and administrative costs are disclosed as a percentage rather than buried.
