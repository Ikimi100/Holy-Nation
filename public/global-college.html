What This Pillar Is

Equipping, Not Entertaining

Zeal without knowledge has cost the Church more than persecution ever has. The Global College of Apostolic Studies exists because the apostolic mandate is not transferred by inspiration alone — it is transferred by instruction, examination, supervision and commissioning.

The College is the teaching engine of Holy Nation Global. Every network, prayer tower, compassion initiative and nation-building project in this movement draws its people from here. What the Apostolic House forms in character, the College forms in competence.

“And He Himself gave some to be apostles, some prophets, some evangelists, and some pastors and teachers, for the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ.” Ephesians 4:11–12

What makes this different from a Bible school

Most training programmes end at information. Ours does not release a certificate until a student has been observed doing the thing they were taught. Every institute carries a practicum, every practicum carries a supervisor, and every supervisor files a written assessment that the academic council reads.

  • Ordered, not optional — institutes are taken in sequence; nobody skips the foundation
  • Assessed — written work, oral examination and a supervised field practicum in every institute
  • Mentored — each student sits under a named mentor from the Apostolic House
  • Accessible — delivered on campus, online and through regional cohorts in four continents
  • Terminal in commissioning — the ladder ends in licensing and deployment, not a framed page

The Ladder

Your Academic Pathway

Six stages from first enquiry to commissioning. Each stage must be completed and assessed before the next opens.

Stage 00 · Prerequisite

Foundations Class

Delivered by the Apostolic House, not the College. Four sessions on the doctrine of Christ, the Ecclesia and personal spiritual disciplines. Required before any institute opens to you.

Stage 01 · 2 terms

Christ Institute

The person, work, offices and supremacy of Christ. Everything downstream depends on getting this right, so it is taught first and examined hardest.

Stage 02 · 2 terms

Kingdom Institute

The Kingdom as a government: its constitution, citizenship, economy and jurisdiction over the twelve spheres of society.

Stage 03 · 2 terms

Apostolic Institute

Apostolic doctrine, order, fathering and the building of houses. The advanced tier, restricted to students who have cleared Stages 01 and 02.

Stage 04 · 2 terms

Ministry Institute

The practical craft: preaching, pastoral care, counselling, administration, ethics and the handling of money and people.

Stage 05 · 6 months

KMTC & Licensing

Residential and field training at the Kingdom Ministers Training Centre, closing with examination, licensing and deployment.

Entry Requirements

  • A clear personal testimony of faith in Christ
  • Completion of the Foundations Class
  • Active membership or partnership with a local church
  • A written reference from your pastor or mentor
  • Reading and writing competence in English
  • Willingness to submit to assessment and correction

No prior academic qualification is required. We have licensed ministers who left school at fifteen and ministers with doctorates. The ladder is the same for both.

Stage 05ResidentialTerminal

Kingdom Ministers Training Centre

The final stage and the gateway to licensing. KMTC is intensive, residential where possible, and deliberately uncomfortable — it exists to test under load what the classroom taught in calm.

Candidates are deployed into real assignments across the movement: prayer towers, mission teams, compassion field work and church plants. Assessment is continuous, and not everyone who enters is licensed at the end.

Components

  • Six-week residential intensive
  • Twelve-week field deployment under a serving minister
  • Cross-pillar rotation through prayer, mission and compassion work
  • Weekly written reflection reviewed by the academic council
  • Final oral examination before the apostolic council
  • Licensing service and deployment

Admissions Process
Credentials

Certification & Licensing

Every award is issued by the academic council and recorded in the College register. Credentials are renewable, reviewable and — where conduct requires it — revocable.

Certificates

Issued on completion of Christ Institute and Kingdom Institute. Confirms doctrinal grounding and qualifies you to teach in house fellowships.

Diplomas

Issued on completion of the Apostolic and Ministry Institutes. Qualifies you for departmental leadership and supervised preaching.

Ministerial License

Issued after KMTC and the oral board. Authorises you to serve as a recognised minister within the movement and its affiliates.

Ordination

Conferred by the apostolic council after a minimum of two years of licensed, assessed service in the field.

How Credentials Are Governed

Who issues and holds the register?

The academic council issues all certificates and diplomas. The apostolic council issues licenses and ordination. Both are recorded in a single register held by the College registrar, and any affiliate church may request verification of a credential.

Do licenses expire?

Ministerial licenses are renewed every two years. Renewal requires evidence of active service, continuing study, and a satisfactory character reference from a supervising minister.

Can a credential be withdrawn?

Yes. Serious doctrinal deviation or moral failure triggers a formal review. Withdrawal is a last resort, always accompanied by a defined restoration pathway, and never used to settle disagreements or protect reputations.

Are these credentials academically accredited?

These are ecclesiastical credentials recognised within Holy Nation Global and its affiliate networks — they are not state academic degrees, and we do not present them as such. Where students require secular accreditation, we advise on partner institutions.

I trained elsewhere. Can I transfer credit?

Prior training is assessed case by case by the academic council. Credit is commonly granted toward Stages 01 and 02. The Apostolic Institute, Ministry Institute and KMTC must be completed with us without exception.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need to be a member of Christhouse to enrol?

No. You do need to be in active membership somewhere and provide a reference from that church's leadership. Many of our students belong to affiliate churches across four continents, and some to churches with no formal relationship to us at all.

How many hours a week should I expect to spend?

Plan for six to eight hours weekly: four contact hours plus reading and written assignments. KMTC is substantially heavier and effectively full-time during the residential phase.

Can I study while working full-time?

Yes — most of our students do. The online and cohort modes exist precisely for this. The one stage that is difficult to combine with full-time employment is KMTC, and we help candidates plan for it well in advance.

What if I fail a module?

You resit it. There is no shame attached and no cap on resits within a reasonable window. What we will not do is advance a student who has not grasped the material, because the cost of that lands on the people they will later lead.

Is the College only for people going into full-time ministry?

Not at all. Roughly half of our students are professionals, business owners and public servants who never intend to pastor. Stages 01 and 02 in particular are designed for every believer, and feed directly into the marketplace mentoring track.

Can women be licensed and ordained through the College?

Yes. Women study the full ladder, sit the same examinations and are licensed on the same terms. The Apostolic Women Fellowship International provides additional mentoring and covering.

Can my church host a regional cohort?

Yes, subject to affiliation through IFAM and a trained, licensed facilitator on site. The College supplies the syllabus, materials and assessment; the host church supplies the venue and pastoral covering.
