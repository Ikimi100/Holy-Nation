Who We Are

The House That Trains East Africa

Kenya carries a particular assignment within this movement. It is not simply another national community — it hosts the East Africa Training Centre, the Global College delivery point serving Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Tanzania and beyond.

That was a deliberate decision, and it reversed a bad old habit. For generations, the promising young leader from Nakuru or Kigali was sent abroad to be trained, and a significant number never came back. Building the training here means people are formed inside the context they will serve, alongside people who face the same conditions.

“And the things that you have heard from me among many witnesses, commit these to faithful men who will be able to teach others also.” 2 Timothy 2:2

Two languages, one house

The Sunday assembly runs in English with Kiswahili woven through worship and prayer, and city groups switch freely between the two. Global College materials are issued in both. In Kisumu and Eldoret, sessions frequently run in Dholuo and Kalenjin where that is how people actually pray.

What we are, and what we are not

  • You keep your church. Most of our members belong to a Kenyan congregation already
  • You get known. Every member is placed in a city group or campus chapter
  • You get trained. The full ladder is delivered here, not exported to you
  • You carry something. Kenya holds Watch 04 on the global roster
  • You are never asked for money to belong. Ever

Our place in the movement

Kenya leads the East Africa sub-region of TKC-Africa, currently in year three of the five-year cycle. Our sub-regional council is forming with an indigenous chair, and full autonomy is due in year five. We work closely with Uganda and Rwanda, who share our training centre and, increasingly, our staff.

Take a slot

Why Kenya holds the Dawn Watch

Watch 04 runs from 3:00 to 6:00 AM — the hardest slot on the roster, and the one traditionally associated with breakthrough. East Africa took it because our time zone means that when we are praying at dawn, it is still the middle of the night across most of the movement.

It is not romantic. It is a genuinely difficult discipline that most people can sustain one morning a week and not more. That is exactly how the roster is designed — one slot, one day, shared with two or three others.

  • Prayed alone, with your household, or in the live audio room
  • You receive the watch guide, the current burden list and a reporting template
  • The Nairobi tower gathers physically for the watch on the first Saturday of each month

SANI Kenya

Six Campus Chapters

The Students Apostolic Network is the fastest-growing part of Holy Nation Kenya, and by some distance the most demanding. Kenyan campuses produce serious questions, serious debate and students who will not accept an answer simply because an older person gave it.

That is the point. A faith that cannot survive a second-year philosophy seminar was never going to survive a mortgage, a bereavement or a corrupt employer either.

  • Weekly fellowships on six campuses, student-led with a licensed adult adviser
  • Apologetics training that takes the objections seriously rather than caricaturing them
  • Career and purpose clinics with KBLN mentors from the relevant field
  • Identity, purity and digital life discussed frankly, without moralising
  • Student-led outreach on and around their own campuses
  • A deliberate handover into a city group at graduation, so nobody drifts away

Campus chapters send delegates to the annual SANI Campus Congress, and several Kenyan students now facilitate regional cohorts of the Global College while still studying.

Campuses With Chapters

  • University of Nairobi
  • Kenyatta University
  • Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture & Technology
  • Moi University, Eldoret
  • Maseno University, Kisumu
  • Technical University of Mombasa

Studying somewhere not listed? Three students and a willing adviser is enough to start a chapter. We supply the curriculum, training and safeguarding cover.

Compassion in Kenya

What We Actually Run

Field programmes with named sites, real caseloads and published numbers. Help is offered on the basis of need alone — no faith test, no attendance requirement.

CES — School Support

Fees, uniforms and books paid directly to schools for children who would otherwise drop out. Attendance reviewed each term.

AFER — Feeding

Community kitchens and school feeding in three counties, run by local volunteers using local suppliers.

HACO — Mobile Clinics

Screening and clinic days in underserved areas, staffed by qualified clinicians with proper onward referral.

SSF — Savings Groups

Member-run savings and lending circles building resilience before crisis, run alongside CIEL skills training.

Safeguarding on every site

Every worker with access to children is screened, trained annually and never alone with a minor. Concerns are escalated the same day to the safeguarding lead and to statutory services where the threshold is met — including concerns about our own people.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I have to leave my church to join Holy Nation Kenya?

No. Most of our members belong to a Kenyan congregation already, and we would discourage anyone from leaving. We sit alongside your church — city groups meet midweek and the training is designed to strengthen you wherever you serve.

What does training at the Centre cost?

Fees are set regionally so they remain proportionate to local income, with instalment plans across each term at no extra cost and bursaries from the Kingdom Builders fund. No student has been turned away for inability to pay, and applying for support carries no weight in the admissions decision.

Is the award from Nairobi the same as from Lagos?

Identical. Same curriculum, same examinations, same assessment standards, same register, same award. The East Africa Training Centre is a delivery centre of the Global College, not a junior version of it.

Do I have to be a Christian to receive help from your programmes?

No. Eligibility is based on need alone. Nobody is asked about their faith, invited to a service as a condition, or treated differently for declining. Charity with conditions attached is not charity.

Can my church host a regional cohort?

Yes, subject to IFAM affiliation and a trained, licensed facilitator on site. We supply the syllabus, materials and assessment; your church supplies the venue and pastoral covering. Cohorts currently run in Kampala, Kigali, Dar es Salaam and Eldoret.

Are you politically affiliated?

No. Holy Nation Global endorses no party and no candidate in any nation, and our groups do not permit campaigning. The full policy is published on the Country Development page and applies here without exception.

Someone asked me to send money in your name. Is that you?

No. Nobody from this movement will ever direct-message you asking for a payment, an M-Pesa transfer, bank details or personal documents. Screenshot it and send it to theholynationglobal@gmail.com so we can warn the community.
