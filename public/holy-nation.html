Welcome Home

One Mandate. Eight Pillars. Every Sphere of Society.

Holy Nation Global is an apostolic movement called to raise leaders, disciple nations, and demonstrate the reign of Christ in every place where human life is lived — the church, the classroom, the marketplace, the family, and the seat of government.

We are not a single congregation. We are a house, a college, a prayer army, a network of networks, a compassion arm, and a nation-building movement — working together as one body under one mandate: Thy Kingdom Come.

Apostolic Foundation

Built on the doctrine of Christ and the pattern of the apostles.

Discipleship That Transforms

Structured training that produces character, competence and commission.

Nations, Not Just Individuals

We disciple cities, states and nations — not merely gather crowds.
